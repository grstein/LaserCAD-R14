LaserCAD - first run
====================

LaserCAD is not code-signed, so your operating system warns the first time
you open it. The app is safe to run; the warning only says that the publisher
is unknown.

Windows (SmartScreen)
  1. Unzip the archive and double-click lasercad.exe.
  2. If "Windows protected your PC" appears, click "More info",
     then "Run anyway".

macOS (Gatekeeper)
  1. Open the .dmg and drag LaserCAD.app to Applications.
  2. Right-click LaserCAD.app, choose Open, then Open again.
     Or, in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LaserCAD.app

The full install guide, including where settings, recent files and autosave
are stored, is docs/install.md:
https://github.com/grstein/LaserCAD-R14-V2/blob/main/docs/install.md
